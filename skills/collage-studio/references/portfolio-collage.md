# Portfolio Collage mode

Use this mode to turn project material into a polished portfolio page or spread with a clear narrative.

## Plan the page

Choose one page message. Build a reading order from headline to project context, process evidence, design decision, and takeaway. Use a grid suitable for the requested format; if none is given, propose a practical aspect ratio before production.

## Compose

- Make one image or idea dominant and keep supporting material subordinate.
- Use captions to explain why each artifact matters, not merely what it depicts.
- Distinguish process documentation, final design, and speculative visualization.
- Keep research evidence close to the design decision it supports.
- Avoid decorative layers that obscure prototypes, diagrams, or legibility.
- Preserve enough empty space for the page to read at thumbnail and presentation scale.

## Handoff

Provide the page or spread, editable title and captions, grid and reading order, source ledger, and a brief critique covering narrative clarity, evidence strength, and the next revision priority.
