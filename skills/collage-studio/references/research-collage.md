# Research Collage mode

Use this mode to organize interviews, observations, artifacts, desk research, and early interpretations into a visual research surface.

## Structure

Organize the collage as a readable argument:

1. context or research question;
2. raw evidence;
3. patterns and tensions;
4. design inference;
5. open questions or next research action.

Use visible provenance markers such as participant codes, dates, locations, artifact numbers, or citation numbers when supplied. Connect evidence to interpretation with lines, numbering, or spatial grouping; do not imply causality from proximity alone.

## Guardrails

- Preserve original wording and context for interview excerpts.
- Keep contradictory evidence visible instead of smoothing it away.
- Treat stock and AI-generated images as contextual or speculative material, never field evidence.
- Replace missing content with labeled research tasks, not invented material.

## Handoff

Provide the collage, a numbered source ledger, verified findings, design inferences, contradictions, and next research questions.
