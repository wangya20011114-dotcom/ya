# Evidence boundaries

Maintain a source ledger for every item used in the collage.

| Label | Meaning | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| `真实调研证据` | Supplied field notes, interview excerpts, original photos, test records, measurements, or cited sources with provenance | May support factual claims; keep the source beside the item or in a numbered note |
| `设计推论` | Interpretation, hypothesis, theme, metaphor, or design opportunity derived from evidence | Phrase as an interpretation, not a finding; link it to supporting evidence where possible |
| `AI 生成素材` | Generated or substantially synthesized imagery or text | Use for atmosphere, scenarios, or concept visualization; never label it as documentary material |
| `来源不明` | Material whose creator, date, context, or rights are not known | Use only as temporary reference; do not treat it as research proof or publish without review |

## Writing rules

- Prefer “这提示我们……” for inference and “受访者原话/现场记录显示……” for verified evidence.
- Keep quotations exact. If a quote is paraphrased, mark it as a summary.
- Do not merge multiple participants into a fictional quotation.
- Do not generate realistic-looking interview screenshots, field photos, charts, or statistics that could be mistaken for actual research.
- When evidence is missing, add a visible placeholder such as `待补：3 位目标用户访谈`.

## Final provenance check

Before handoff, confirm that each factual statement has a source, every generated element is labeled, and every inference is phrased as a hypothesis or design judgment.
